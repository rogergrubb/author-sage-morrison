Signed Copies

Direct from Sage. By mail. About 14 days.

If you’d like a copy signed by hand, I’d be glad to send you one. Fill in the form below. Your request comes directly to me. I’ll sign your book, write whatever inscription you ask for (within reason — I’ll politely decline things I can’t put my name to), package it carefully, and put it in the mail.

Timing. Allow about 14 days from when you submit this form to when the book arrives at your door. Most orders go out within the first week; the rest are postal time. If something goes sideways I will email you.

Payment. I’ll send you a short email with a payment link (Stripe or PayPal — your choice) and confirm the shipping cost for your address before I post the book. No pre-payment through this form; no card details collected here.
